How it works

One phone. The whole room.

Your phone guides the event, the TV sets the stage, and every guest takes part from their own phone, all perfectly in sync.

1

The host console

The run of show, check-in, games, moments, raffles and scores in one place, with separate sign-ins for your team.

2

The big screen

Any TV with a web browser becomes the stage for questions, countdowns, a live leaderboard, photo slideshows and a confetti finale.

3

Every guest's phone

One scan and guests are in, straight from their browser. They play, vote, hunt and share photos all evening.
