Door 2 · Planners & venues

Every client's event, in one place.

Give each client a simple private link, keep your team on track, and run every event the same dependable way. Start with one client. Add the deeper tools only when you want them.

A portal for every client

One link, no account. Your client sees their checklist, what is waiting on them, their dates, their payments and their messages. You choose what they see.

Checklists you reuse

Save your usual wedding, birthday or corporate checklist once. Add a new client and the due dates fill in from the event date.

Approvals with a record

Send a menu, floor plan or design. The client taps Approve or Request changes, and you keep the history.

Uploads that arrive ready

Clients send photos for printed boards, PDFs and guest lists. You review a guest list before it changes yours.

A team that stays on track

Add teammates. Each sees only their own tasks, across all your events, and you can remind everyone who is behind. On the day, they get a simple “do this now” screen with a help button.

Payments, dates and vendors

Track what is paid and due, key dates with add-to-calendar, and every vendor's phone and arrival time. Share them with clients and teammates, or keep them private.

Your brand, their portal

Your logo, name and colour on every client and teammate page, with a weekly summary you can post in one tap.

Feedback you can use

After the event, clients and guests rate it and tell you what worked. Feature the comments they allow as testimonials.

What it does, and what it doesn't

Does
  • Keeps vendors, payments, dates, files and approvals in one place
  • Shows clients and teammates exactly what you decide to share
  • Shows reminders and weekly summaries inside each portal
Doesn't
  • Book vendors for you
  • Take or send money. Payments are tracked, not processed
  • Email reminders yet. They show inside the portal for now
