Three screens, one event

You run it. The room feels it.

Your phone is the remote. The TV is the stage. Every guest's phone is a game controller. Tap once and they all move together.

1

The host's phone

Your console for the whole day: the run of show, check-in, games, moments, raffles and scores. Helpers get their own sign-in.

2

The big screen

Any TV with a browser. Questions, countdowns, the live leaderboard, photo slideshows and a gold-confetti finale.

3

Every guest's phone

Guests scan one code and they're in. No app, no account. They answer, react, hunt and share photos.
